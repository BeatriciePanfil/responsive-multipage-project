Medicube

Medicube is a leading South Korean derm-cosmetic brand launched under APR Corp that focuses on bringing clinical-grade, results-driven skincare into everyday routines. The brand is globally recognized for fusing bioactive ingredients with advanced skin-tech. Its comprehensive skincare lines are formulated alongside dermatologists to solve targeted concerns like enlarged pores, acne, and loss of elasticity.

SKIN1004

SKIN1004 is a popular Korean skincare brand renowned for harnessing the calming power of Centella Asiatica harvested from the untouched nature of Madagascar. Their minimalist formulas focus on pure, gentle, and effective ingredients designed to soothe irritation, reduce redness, and strengthen the skin barrier. Ideal for sensitive and acne-prone skin, products like their signature Madagascar Centella Ampoule provide lightweight hydration without any heavy or sticky residue.

Dr.Althea

Dr. Althea is a premium Korean derma-skincare brand launched by founder Evelyn Lee, named after the Greek goddess of truth and sincerity. The vegan and cruelty-free brand blends advanced scientific research with clean, natural ingredients to create gentle formulas specifically optimized for sensitive, acne-prone, and barrier-stressed skin. Acclaimed globally for its problem-solving philosophy and elegant aesthetic, its lineup is anchored by highly celebrated staples like the viral Vitamin C Boosting Serum and the ultra-soothing 345 Relief Cream.
